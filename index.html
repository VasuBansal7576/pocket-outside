Choose somewhere you already know is suitable. This app does not check weather, air quality, access or route safety. Observe wildlife without approaching it.

How the AI chooses

A quantized MiniLM model turns your words and these original activity descriptions into normalized sentence embeddings. Cosine similarity ranks the activities that fit your checked surroundings, time and one-spot preference. The model retrieves a written card; it never invents a route or wildlife advice. Similarity is not a confidence or safety score.

MiniLM-L6-v2, Apache 2.0 weights. Transformers.js 3.8.1 and ONNX Runtime, WASM on CPU.
